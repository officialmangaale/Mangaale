Account Deletion Request

App: Mangaale Partner (Android package com.mangaale.restaurant)
Effective date: 30 August 2026

This page explains how users of the Mangaale Partner mobile application can request deletion of their app account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion from inside the app at Profile → Delete account. That screen also provides an email-request option. To request deletion without installing the app:

  1. Send an email to supportmangaale@gmail.com with the subject line Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the account email address, phone number, restaurant name, and any other identifying details needed to verify ownership of the account.
  3. We may contact you to verify your identity before processing the deletion request.

If the in-app request cannot proceed because the account has an active subscription or an unpaid balance, follow the instructions shown under Billing & Subscription or contact support.

What Will Be Deleted

  • App account profile information and sign-in access.
  • Stored authentication records associated with the app account.
  • Restaurant profile data managed through the app, where deletion is permitted and not subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Uploaded business images and verification documents.
  • Menu, inventory, vendor, referral, staff, and related settings data.
  • Notification tokens and in-app operational preferences.

What May Be Retained

Certain data may be retained where required for legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or security purposes. This may include invoice, payment, order, audit, or compliance records that must be preserved under applicable law or legitimate business obligations.

For books of account and relevant vouchers this may mean eight financial years where applicable under Section 128(5) of the Companies Act, 2013.

Processing Time

We process verified deletion requests as soon as reasonably practicable. The time required depends on identity verification, account status, the data involved, operational processes, and applicable legal requirements.

When personal information becomes eligible for deletion, we will delete, anonymise or restrict that information in accordance with our operational deletion processes, subject to applicable legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, dispute-resolution, regulatory and backup-retention requirements. Residual copies of information may temporarily remain in backups until those backups are overwritten or expire according to our applicable backup-retention procedures.

Partial Data Deletion

At this time, Mangaale Partner does not provide a separate self-service process for deleting only selected categories of data without also requesting account deletion, unless otherwise required by law.

Contact

For deletion requests or questions, contact supportmangaale@gmail.com.
